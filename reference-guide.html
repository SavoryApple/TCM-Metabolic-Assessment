Endocrine Square Assessment Patterns

The Endocrine Square evaluates the relationship between thyroid, adrenal, blood sugar, and reproductive hormones to identify systemic endocrine dysfunction patterns.

Pattern 1: Pure Hypofunction (All Underactive)

Subsections: 6A + 6B + 6C + 6D (all high scores)

TCM Pattern: Severe Kidney Yang Deficiency, Spleen Yang Deficiency, Qi & Blood Deficiency

Clinical Presentation: Profound fatigue, cold intolerance, weight gain, depression, low libido, poor concentration

Key Tests: Complete Thyroid Panel, DUTCH Complete, Fasting Glucose/Insulin, Full Hormone Panel (FSH, LH, Estradiol, Progesterone, Testosterone)

Estimated Cost: $700+

Pattern 2: Pure Hyperfunction (All Overactive)

Subsections: 7A + 7B + 7C + 7D (all high scores)

TCM Pattern: Liver Fire, Yin Deficiency with Heat, Dampness-Heat

Clinical Presentation: Anxiety, insomnia, heat intolerance, weight gain (central), high blood pressure, mood swings

Key Tests: Thyroid Panel + TSI, 4-Point Cortisol, Fasting Glucose/Insulin/HbA1c, DUTCH Complete, Lipid Panel

Estimated Cost: $700+
